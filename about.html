Sam Cox has five years of hands-on experience managing feral animal populations across NSW — from open grazing country to timbered hill country to riparian zones. He founded Stateline to do one thing properly: licensed, effective feral animal control, delivered personally.

He holds every licence and accreditation required to operate legally in NSW — the full register is below, codes and all — and he carries out every job himself. When you hire Stateline, Sam shows up. Not a subcontractor. Not a rotating crew. The operator you spoke to on the phone.

That structure isn't a limitation — it's the point. One standard of work, one point of accountability, and nothing lost between the person you call and the person on the ground.
