3Wild Dog Control

Stock losses that hit hard and fast — especially against timbered country.

Wild dogs, dingoes and hybrids cause some of the most severe stock losses in NSW — killing and mauling sheep, goats and calves, often well beyond what's actually eaten. Properties bordering timbered hill country and reserves carry constant boundary pressure.

Stateline runs targeted wild dog programs for affected properties, built around where the pressure is coming from and where stock is most exposed.

Rooting, fouled water, wrecked fences — and a genuine biosecurity risk.

Feral pigs tear up pasture and crops, foul dams and waterways, damage fencing, and prey on lambs. Beyond the visible damage, they're recognised as one of the most significant biosecurity risks on Australian farmland.

Stateline runs pig control programs across NSW farming and grazing country, from seasonal response to sustained programs on properties under ongoing pressure.

6Kangaroo Management

A protected native species that still needs active, regulated management.

Kangaroo numbers can build quickly on grazing country, particularly through dry seasons, competing directly with livestock for pasture and putting pressure on fencing and water points. Left unmanaged, that competition compounds fast.

Kangaroos are a protected native species, so this work is different to feral animal control — it's regulated under the NSW Kangaroo Management Program and requires a specific Occupier's Licence. Stateline's licence for this program is currently in progress; the accreditation code will be added here once it's issued. Call Sam to discuss timing and what's involved.

ProtectsPasture reserved for stock, fencing, water points
PressureGrazing competition, seasonal population surges

7Rabbit & Hare Control

Small, fast-breeding, and relentless on pasture.

Rabbits and hares dig in fast and multiply faster, tearing up pasture, undermining fence lines and creating erosion through warren networks. On grazing country they compete directly with stock for feed, and numbers can build unnoticed until the ground damage is already done.

Stateline runs rabbit and hare control across NSW properties, often alongside fox programs since the two species interact closely on the same ground. Covered under the same licensing Sam already holds — no separate accreditation required.
